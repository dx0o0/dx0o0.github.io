研究方向Research

围绕可信的数据生产、管理与使用，研究数据质量如何支撑分析系统与机器学习任务。Research on trustworthy data production, management, and use for analytical systems and machine-learning tasks.

研究贯穿数据治理、质量评估、错误检测与修复、数据清洗系统、工业时序数据管理和面向 AI 的数据准备，重点关注数据侧决策的效果、成本与可复用性。

The work spans data governance, quality assessment, error detection and repair, data-cleaning systems, industrial time-series management, and data preparation for AI, with an emphasis on effectiveness, cost, and reusable data-side decisions.

01

大数据治理Big Data Governance

研究数据资产价值、数据流通与治理活动之间的关系，形成面向组织与产业场景的数据管理方法。

Studies the relationship among data-asset value, data circulation, and governance activities for organizational and industrial settings.

02

数据质量管理Data Quality Management

关注数据质量评估、错误检测与修复、清洗方案推荐，以及多类型错误的统一处理流程。

Focuses on quality assessment, error detection and repair, cleaning-solution recommendation, and unified workflows for mixed errors.

03

面向机器学习的高质量数据构建High-quality Data for ML

分析数据清洗、样本选择与下游模型之间的作用关系，在成本受限条件下联合优化数据与模型。

Connects cleaning and sample selection with downstream models, jointly optimizing data and models under practical cost constraints.

04

工业物联网时序数据Industrial IoT Time Series

研究工业时序数据的质量规则、异常检测、依赖发现、清洗与高鲁棒查询处理。

Studies quality rules, anomaly detection, dependency discovery, cleaning, and robust query processing for industrial time series.
