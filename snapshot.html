关于我About

丁小欧，哈尔滨工业大学计算学部副教授、博士生导师，于哈尔滨工业大学获得本科和博士学位。研究方向为大数据治理、数据质量管理、面向机器学习的高质量数据构建，以及工业物联网时序数据治理与分析。

Xiaoou Ding is an Associate Professor and Ph.D. supervisor at the Faculty of Computing, Harbin Institute of Technology, where she received both her bachelor's and doctoral degrees. Research interests include big data governance, data quality management, high-quality data construction for machine learning, and industrial IoT time-series data governance and analytics.

简历记载已在 IEEE TKDE、VLDB、ICDE、CIKM 等国际重要期刊和会议发表论文 30 余篇，在《软件学报》《大数据》等国内高水平期刊发表论文 10 余篇，并参与编写专著和教材 4 部。

Her CV records more than 30 papers in major international journals and conferences, including IEEE TKDE, VLDB, ICDE, and CIKM, more than 10 papers in leading Chinese journals, and contributions to four books and textbooks.

研究兴趣Research Interests

  • 大数据治理与数据资产价值评估。Big data governance and data-asset value assessment.
  • 数据质量评估、错误检测与修复、数据清洗系统。Data quality assessment, error detection and repair, and data-cleaning systems.
  • 面向机器学习的数据准备、样本选择及数据—模型协同优化。Data preparation, sample selection, and data-model co-optimization for machine learning.
  • 工业物联网时序数据的质量规则、依赖发现与高鲁棒处理。Quality rules, dependency discovery, and robust processing for industrial IoT time series.
